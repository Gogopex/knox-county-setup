# Sources

Workshop content remains with its authors. Every upstream folder in `integration.json` records its Workshop item ID; patch operations record their source paths and checksums. Patch fragments are modifications to those upstream works, not a claim of authorship over the originals.

ZombieBuddy: https://github.com/zed-0xff/ZombieBuddy. Its official Windows installer is downloaded directly from the author's release and checked against the recorded SHA-256. Its runtime is installed from subscribed Workshop content. Its license and approval prompts remain upstream-managed.

Companion Dogs bite contact foley uses three edited excerpts of "Knife Stab.wav" by neilsher, CC0 1.0: https://freesound.org/people/neilsher/sounds/411743/. The integration includes the credits file alongside those sound files.

The MIT license in this repository covers the setup-manager code and tests. It does not relicense third-party mods or patch fragments.
